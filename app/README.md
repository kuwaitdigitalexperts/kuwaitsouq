# KuwaitSouq Mobile App (Flutter)

KuwaitSouq is a high-performance cross-platform classifieds marketplace application tailored for Kuwait and GCC countries (Kuwait 🇰🇼, Saudi Arabia 🇸🇦, UAE 🇦🇪, Qatar 🇶🇦, Bahrain 🇧🇭, Oman 🇴🇲).

## Features
- **GCC Multi-Country & City Browsing**: Real-time country/city switcher and first-launch country selector.
- **Dynamic Automotive & General Categories**: Custom quick-brand carousels, dynamic filter pills, and search by keywords.
- **CarFax & Kayishha (كايشها)**: Integrated vehicle history inquiry and instant car evaluation modals.
- **Real-Time Buyer-Seller Chat**: Fast in-app direct messaging with ad linking and pre-filled quick questions.
- **Favorites & Boost Ad**: Save favorite ads and boost listings with GCC payment methods (KNET, Apple Pay, Visa, MasterCard).
- **Phone & Social Authentication**: Firebase Phone Auth OTP ready with backend Sanctum authentication.
